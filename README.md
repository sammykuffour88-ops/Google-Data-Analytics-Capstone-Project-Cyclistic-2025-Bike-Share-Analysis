# Google-Data-Analytics-Capstone-Project-Cyclistic-2025-Bike-Share-Analysis
Google Data Analytics Capstone Project – Cyclistic 2025 Bike-Share Analysis
Cyclistic 2025 Bike-Share Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses Cyclistic bike-share data from January to December 2025 to understand how casual riders and annual members use the service differently.

The goal is to identify opportunities to encourage casual riders to become annual members through data-driven marketing recommendations.

Business Questions

1. How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?
2. Why would casual riders buy Cyclistic annual memberships?
3. How can Cyclistic use digital media to influence casual riders to become members?

Tools Used

* Google BigQuery
* SQL
* Google Sheets
* Tableau Public
* Microsoft Word

Data Preparation

The original dataset contained 5,552,994 rides. After data checks and cleaning, 5,547,380 valid rides remained.

Key Findings

* Annual members accounted for 64.04% of recorded rides.
* Casual riders had longer average rides than members.
* Casual riders were relatively more active at weekends.
* Both groups recorded their highest ride numbers in August.
* Electric bikes were popular with both groups.

Recommendations

* Target casual riders with weekend promotions.
* Promote memberships before and during summer.
* Use location-based digital advertising.
* Highlight electric bike benefits.
* Test personalised digital marketing campaigns.

Conclusion

The analysis identified differences in riding behaviour between casual riders and annual members. These findings provide opportunities for Cyclistic to develop targeted marketing strategies to encourage casual riders to become annual members.
